## 2. What Information We Collect

### 2A. Information You Provide Directly

**Account Information**
When you create an account, we collect:
- Your email address (used for login and account recovery only)
- A password (stored as an encrypted hash — we never store your actual password)
- Your optional Meadow display name (a name you choose; does not have to be your real name)
- Your optional botanical avatar selection

We do not require your legal name, phone number, date of birth, physical address, or any government-issued identification to use The Meadow.

**Journal Entries**
When you write in the Journal, we collect and store:
- The text of your journal entries
- The mood you selected (Heavy, Tender, Okay, Quiet, Hopeful, or Numb)
- The date and time the entry was created
- The chapter or ritual context if you entered the Journal from a chapter screen

Your journal entries are private. They are stored encrypted in your account. No member of The Meadow team reads your journal entries. The AI prompt feature (described below) does NOT send your written entries to any AI service — it sends only your selected mood, your most recent chapter, and your most recent ritual, which are used to generate a writing prompt.

**Community Content**
When you participate in The Hearth (community spaces), we collect:
- Letters you write in The Post Office
- Messages you send in Tea Rooms
- Posts and comments you make in The Courtyard
- Healing circles and workshops you join in The Greenhouse
- Reactions and seals you give to other users' content
- Reports you file about content or other users

Community content is visible to other users of The Meadow as intended by the features. Please do not share personally identifying information (your real name, location, phone number, etc.) in community spaces.

**Subscription Information**
If you subscribe to The Greenhouse (subscriber-only features), payment is processed by **Stripe**, a third-party payment processor. We do not receive or store your credit card number, debit card number, or full payment details. We receive from Stripe:
- Confirmation that a subscription is active
- Your subscription plan type and renewal date
- A Stripe customer ID (a reference number, not payment data)

**Check-Ins and Progress**
When you use The Greenhouse check-in or Water the Garden features, we store:
- Your selected mood for that check-in
- The date you checked in
- Whether you watered the garden on a given day

**Companion and Chapter Data**
We store your progress through the app locally on your device (using AsyncStorage) and, when you are logged in, sync it to your account. This includes:
- Which rituals you have completed and when
- Which chapters you have visited
- Which Witnessing Companions have appeared for you and how many times
- The witnessed notes you have unlocked

### 2B. Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information**
When you use the app, we automatically receive basic technical information including:
- Device type (iPhone, Android phone, tablet)
- Operating system version
- App version number
- General error logs (crash reports) if the app encounters a problem

We do not collect your precise GPS location. We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library unless you explicitly choose to upload a photo (a feature, if added, will request your explicit permission first).

**Usage Information**
We collect basic analytics about how the app is used, such as:
- Which screens are visited
- Which features are used
- Session length

This data is collected in aggregate and anonymized where possible. It is used to improve the app experience and to understand which features are valuable to users. It is not used to build an advertising profile or to target you with ads.

## 3. Where We Store Your Data

### Primary Storage: Supabase

All account data, journal entries, community content, and app progress data is stored in **Supabase**, a cloud database platform. Supabase stores data on servers located in the **United States**, operated by Amazon Web Services (AWS).

Supabase is SOC 2 Type 2 compliant and uses industry-standard encryption:
- **Data in transit**: encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher
- **Data at rest**: encrypted using AES-256

Supabase's privacy policy is available at: https://supabase.com/privacy

### Local Storage: Your Device

Your chapter progress, companion data, and certain app preferences are also stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted unless you are logged in to an account, at which point it syncs to Supabase.

### Payment Data: Stripe

Subscription payment data is stored by **Stripe, Inc.**, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. We never touch your payment card data. Stripe's privacy policy is available at: https://stripe.com/privacy

### AI Prompt Service: Anthropic

When you tap "Get a Prompt" in the Journal, a short context message is sent to **Anthropic's API** (the makers of Claude) to generate a writing prompt. This message contains only:
- Your selected mood
- Your most recent chapter name
- Your most recent ritual name
- Your most recent companion name (if applicable)
- The time of day

It does **not** contain your journal text, your email address, your display name, or any other identifying information. Anthropic's privacy policy is available at: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy

## 6. Who We Share Data With

We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

**Service Providers**
We share data with third-party services that help us operate the app:
- **Supabase** — database and authentication
- **Stripe** — subscription payment processing
- **Anthropic** — AI writing prompt generation (anonymized context only, as described above)
- **Expo / EAS** — app building and delivery
- **Apple App Store / Google Play Store** — app distribution (subject to their own privacy policies)

Each of these providers is contractually required to protect your data and use it only to provide services to us.

**Safety and Legal Requirements**
We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to:
- Comply with a valid legal obligation, court order, or government request
- Protect the safety of any person, including in response to a credible threat of harm
- Protect the rights and property of The Meadow
- Investigate or address fraud or security issues

**Crisis Content**
If community content suggests an imminent risk of harm to self or others, our moderation team may take action to surface crisis resources to the user and, in cases of credible imminent danger, may share information with emergency services. We will always attempt to connect users with professional crisis support as our first response.

**Business Transfers**
If The Meadow is acquired by or merged with another entity, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify users by email and in-app notice before any such transfer, and you will have the opportunity to delete your account and data before the transfer occurs.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Portability
You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you at any time by emailing brittanylpike@gmail.com.

### Correction
You may correct your email address, display name, or avatar through the app's Profile settings.

### Deletion
You may delete your account and all associated data at any time using the "Clear Meadow data" option in Profile > Settings. This action permanently deletes:
- Your account credentials
- Your journal entries
- Your community posts, letters, and messages
- Your progress data (chapters, companions, rituals)
- Your subscription record (note: active subscriptions must also be cancelled through the App Store or Google Play to stop future billing)

We retain backups for up to 30 days after deletion for disaster recovery purposes, after which your data is permanently removed from all systems.

### Opt-Out of Analytics
You may contact us at brittanylpike@gmail.com to request that your account be excluded from aggregate usage analytics.

### California Residents (CCPA)
California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete it, the right to opt out of the sale of it (we do not sell it), and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To exercise these rights, contact brittanylpike@gmail.com.

### European Users (GDPR)
If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have rights under the GDPR including the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to object. Our legal basis for processing your data is (a) contract performance (providing the app you signed up for), (b) legitimate interests (improving app safety and performance), and (c) your consent (for optional features like AI prompts). To exercise GDPR rights, contact brittanylpike@gmail.com.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable and industry-standard steps to protect your data, including:
- TLS encryption for all data in transit
- AES-256 encryption for data at rest (via Supabase)
- Hashed and salted password storage (we never store plaintext passwords)
- Access controls limiting which team members can access user data
- Regular review of security practices

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take the protection of your data seriously and will notify you promptly if a breach affecting your data occurs.
