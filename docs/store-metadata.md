## Full Description
The Meadow is a gentle wellness app for anyone navigating grief.
Inspired by the beauty of the natural world and the five stages of
grief, The Meadow guides you through five healing chapters — each
holding five rituals designed to be done at your own pace.

Inside The Meadow you'll find:
• A private journal with mood tracking and guided writing prompts
• Five healing chapters with 25 guided rituals
• A Memory Garden to honour those you've lost
• A Keepsake Box for photos and memories
• The Hearth — a safe community to gather and share
• Crisis resources and support, always one tap away

Grief is not a race. The Meadow will be here.
