## IV. Artificial Intelligence Disclosures

The Meadow utilizes artificial intelligence technology provided by Anthropic, PBC ("Anthropic") to generate personalized journal writing prompts. The following disclosures apply to this feature:

(a) **Scope of AI Input Data.** The AI writing prompt feature transmits to Anthropic's API only the following non-identifying contextual data: the user's selected mood category (from a fixed list of six options), the name of the user's most recently visited chapter, the name of the user's most recently completed ritual, the name of the companion most recently witnessed, and the general time of day. The user's written journal entries are NOT transmitted to Anthropic's API or any other AI system at any time.

(b) **Nature of AI Output.** AI-generated writing prompts are produced by a large language model and are not reviewed by a licensed mental health professional before delivery to the user. They are creative in nature and are intended solely to invite personal reflection. They do not constitute clinical assessments, therapeutic interventions, psychological evaluations, or professional recommendations of any kind.

(c) **AI Limitations.** Artificial intelligence language models, including those used by The Meadow, may produce output that is inaccurate, inappropriate, or potentially distressing to certain users. The Meadow does not warrant the accuracy, appropriateness, or safety of AI-generated content for any individual user's circumstances. Users are encouraged to disregard any AI-generated prompt that feels unhelpful, distressing, or inconsistent with their needs.

(d) **No Diagnostic or Therapeutic Function.** The AI feature incorporated into The Meadow has not been reviewed, approved, authorized, or cleared by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) as a Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) or for any clinical, diagnostic, or therapeutic purpose. It is a creative writing tool only.
