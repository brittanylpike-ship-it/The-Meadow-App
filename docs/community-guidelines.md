# Community Guidelines
**The Meadow — A Living Storybook Journey**
**Effective Date:** June 19, 2026
**Last Updated:** June 19, 2026

---

## Why These Guidelines Exist

The Meadow's community spaces — The Post Office, Tea Rooms, The Greenhouse, and The Courtyard — exist because grief and healing are not meant to be carried alone. People arrive here in real pain. Some are grieving. Some are overwhelmed. Some are on the edge of something difficult. The words you leave in this space can land very heavily on someone you will never see.

These guidelines are not rules designed to restrict you. They are promises we make to each other about the kind of space we want this to be.

**The standard is simple: would you say this to someone sitting next to you at a quiet table, who just told you they were struggling? If not, don't say it here.**

---

## The Foundation: Four Agreements

Every person in The Meadow community agrees to these four things:

### 1. Be Kind
Kindness is not agreement. You do not have to agree with someone to treat them with dignity. Words here land differently than they do in other places online — people are often raw and open when they come here. Write as if you know that.

### 2. Be Honest
Speak from your own experience. Say "I feel" and "for me" — not "you should" or "everyone knows." Your truth is valid. Other people's truths, which may be different from yours, are also valid.

### 3. Hold Space
Holding space means being present without trying to fix. It means listening without immediately offering solutions. In most of The Meadow's community spaces, especially Tea Rooms, the etiquette is: no advice unless someone explicitly asks for it. Someone who says "I'm struggling today" may not want solutions. They may just want to be heard.

### 4. Protect This Space
This community only works because people feel safe enough to be honest. Your actions either build that safety or erode it. If you see something that breaks these guidelines, please report it. Protecting the community is everyone's responsibility.

## What Is Welcome Here

- Sharing how you are actually feeling, without performance or pressure to be "okay"
- Asking for support, company, or a kind word
- Celebrating small wins — getting out of bed, drinking water, making it through a hard day
- Sharing creative work — writing, art, reflection — that comes from your journey
- Asking questions about grief, loss, anxiety, and difficult emotions
- Recommending books, practices, or experiences that have helped you (personal experience, not prescriptions)
- Introducing yourself, wherever you are in your story
- Saying "me too" to someone who needed to hear it

---

## What Is Not Welcome Here

### Content That Harms Others
- Bullying, mocking, targeting, or belittling any user
- Deliberate triggering — posting content designed to destabilize or provoke another user's mental health
- Comparing grief or pain ("at least you didn't...," "that's not as bad as...")
- Unsolicited advice, especially about medication, therapy, or medical decisions
- Dismissing someone's experience ("you'll get over it," "it could be worse," "just think positive")

### Content That Harms the Space
- Spam, advertising, or promotion of products, services, or external communities
- Sharing personal identifying information about other users without their consent
- Spreading misinformation about mental health treatment, medications, or crisis resources
- Content that is sexually explicit, violent, or deliberately disturbing
- Impersonating Meadow Keepers (moderators), the app team, or other users

### Content That Harms Vulnerable People
- Detailed descriptions of methods of self-harm or suicide (even framed as a personal story)
- Content that glorifies, romanticizes, or encourages self-harm, eating disorders, or substance use
- Hateful content targeting any person or group based on race, ethnicity, religion, national origin, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any other characteristic
- Any content involving minors in a sexual or harmful context

## Specific Guidelines by Space

### The Post Office (Letters)
- Letters are read by strangers who came here for the same reason you did.
- Reply to letters the way you would want someone to reply to yours on a hard day.
- "Sealing" a letter means: this letter mattered to me. Use it genuinely.
- If you disagree with something someone shared, you may choose not to engage. You may not attack them for sharing it.

### Tea Rooms (Live Chat)
- **No advice unless asked.** This is the most important rule in Tea Rooms. If someone vents, respond with presence, not solutions.
- What is shared in Tea Rooms stays in Tea Rooms. Do not screenshot or share other users' messages outside the app.
- If you need to step away from a conversation that is affecting you, use the "Rest on the Bench" button. You can return whenever you are ready.
- The "Pour a Cup," "Send a Hug," "Take a Breath" reactions are the language of this space. Use them freely.

### The Greenhouse (Subscriber Circles and Workshops)
- Healing circles are facilitated spaces. Follow the facilitator's guidance.
- Confidentiality applies: do not share what specific people say in circles outside the circle.
- Workshops are spaces for learning, not debate. Questions are welcome; arguments are not.
- The "Ask a Guide" feature is for questions and gentle guidance — it is not a crisis service. If you are in crisis, use 988 or Crisis Text Line.

### The Courtyard (Community Feed)
- Celebrate others' milestones, even when yours feel far away.
- Daily Prompt responses are personal. Respond to others' answers with curiosity, not judgment.
- The Kindness Jar and Gratitude Wall are communal spaces — small, gentle, honest contributions only.
- Polls and community questions are for connection and curiosity, not debate.

## Crisis Content

If you or someone in the community appears to be in immediate danger of harm to themselves or others:

1. **Use the "Report a Concern" button** in the app immediately.
2. Our moderation team will respond and surface crisis resources.
3. In cases of credible imminent danger, we may contact emergency services.

**Please do not post detailed descriptions of suicidal plans, self-harm methods, or active crisis situations in community spaces.** If you are in crisis yourself, please reach out to:
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**: call or text 988 (United States, 24/7)
- **Crisis Text Line**: text HOME to 741741 (24/7)
- **International Association for Suicide Prevention**: https://www.iasp.info/resources/Crisis_Centres/

You can also find crisis resources in the app under Profile → If You Need More.

## Meadow Keepers (Moderators)

Meadow Keepers are community moderators who help maintain the safety and warmth of The Hearth spaces. They may:
- Pin important posts or letters
- Remove content that violates these guidelines
- Issue warnings to users
- Remove users who repeatedly or seriously violate these guidelines

Meadow Keepers are identified in community spaces. Do not impersonate them.

Moderation decisions are made in good faith. If you believe a decision was incorrect, you may appeal by emailing brittanylpike@gmail.com with the subject line "Moderation Appeal."

---

## Reporting

Every community space has reporting tools:
- **Report Letter** — in The Post Office
- **Report a Concern** — in Tea Rooms
- **Report** — on any post or comment in The Courtyard

When you report content, you are not attacking the person who posted it. You are protecting the community. Reports are reviewed by Meadow Keepers. You may also report by emailing brittanylpike@gmail.com.

You can block any user from the same report menu. Blocked users will not appear in your feeds and cannot interact with your content.

---

## Consequences

Violations of these guidelines may result in:
- Content removal
- A private warning
- Temporary suspension from community spaces
- Permanent account termination

Severity depends on the nature and frequency of the violation. Content that threatens safety will be addressed immediately and without warning.
