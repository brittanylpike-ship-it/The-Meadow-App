# Hearth - Model Decisions (confirmed June 20, 2026)

## Subscription
- Subscribers get full Hearth access: The Post Office, Tea Rooms, The Greenhouse, and The Courtyard.
- Free user access model: B confirmed. The Hearth landing is visible, and all four spaces are locked until subscription.
- The subscription gate lives only on the Hearth landing screen. Individual Hearth rooms do not perform their own subscription checks.

## Moderation
- Layer 1: AI moderation runs automatically before community content is published.
- Layer 2: Human review is handled by subscriber moderators with the `meadow_keeper` role.
- No professional moderators or paid staff at launch.

## Grief Support in Hearth
- External reference links only, with the same philosophy as the Profile screen.
- No peer counselors, no trained support staff, no in-app crisis chat.
- Users opt to follow links outside the app on their own terms.
- Crisis resources (988, Crisis Text Line) surface in the safety bar only.
